What We Do

Our Services

End-to-end sea freight solutions tailored for Indian exporters and importers — from booking to final delivery.

🚢

FCL — Full Container Load

Dedicated 20ft and 40ft container bookings for large volume shipments. Ideal for bulk cargo requiring exclusive container space with direct routing.

  • 20ft & 40ft standard containers
  • 20ft & 40ft high cube containers
  • Reefer & special equipment
  • Competitive ocean freight rates
  • Direct & transhipment routing
📦

LCL — Less Container Load

Consolidated groupage shipments for smaller cargo volumes. Share container space and only pay for what you ship — perfect for SMEs and frequent smaller consignments.

  • Weekly consolidation services
  • Competitive CBM-based pricing
  • Cargo insurance options
  • Flexible dispatch schedules
  • CFS stuffing & unstuffing
🛃

Customs Clearance

Expert customs brokerage services for both import and export shipments. We handle all documentation, duty calculations, and regulatory compliance on your behalf.

  • Import & export customs filing
  • Bill of Entry preparation
  • Duty drawback assistance
  • GST & IGST documentation
  • DGFT & FSSAI compliance
📄

Freight Documentation

Accurate and timely preparation of all shipping documents to ensure smooth transit and hassle-free clearance at origin and destination ports.

  • Bill of Lading (OBL & Telex)
  • Packing list & commercial invoice
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Fumigation certificates
  • Phytosanitary certificates
🏭

Inland Transportation

Reliable door-to-port and port-to-door trucking across Rajasthan and North India. We coordinate seamless movement from your factory to the vessel.

  • Factory stuffing / destuffing
  • ICD to port transportation
  • Jodhpur to Mundra / JNPT / Chennai
  • ODC & project cargo
  • Refrigerated transport available
🔒

Cargo Insurance

Comprehensive marine cargo insurance to protect your goods throughout the voyage. We help you choose the right cover for every shipment type and value.

  • Institute Cargo Clauses A / B / C
  • All-risk comprehensive cover
  • War & SRCC cover available
  • Claim assistance support
  • Competitive premium rates

How It Works

From Quote to Delivery

A simple, transparent 5-step process that takes the complexity out of international shipping.

📋
Step 1
Request a Quote
Share your cargo details, origin, and destination. We send you a competitive, itemised freight quote within 24 hours.
✅
Step 2
Booking & Confirmation
Once you approve the quote, we place the booking with the shipping line and confirm your space on the vessel.
📦
Step 3
Cargo Pickup & Stuffing
We coordinate inland transport from your premises to the port or ICD. Container stuffing is supervised and documented.
🛃
Step 4
Export Customs & Docs
We file all shipping bill documents, handle customs examination, and prepare your complete export document set.
🌊
Step 5
Sailing & Delivery
Your cargo sails. We share the Bill of Lading, track the vessel, and coordinate delivery or destination customs at the other end.
