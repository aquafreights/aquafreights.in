What We Do

Our Services

End-to-end sea freight solutions — from factory gate in Jodhpur to port of discharge anywhere in the world.

Sea Freight

LCL — Less Container Load

Consolidated groupage shipments for smaller volumes. Share container space and only pay for what you ship — ideal for SMEs and frequent smaller consignments.

  • Weekly consolidation services
  • Competitive CBM-based pricing
  • CFS stuffing & unstuffing
  • Flexible dispatch schedules
Sea Freight

FCL — Full Container Load

Dedicated 20ft and 40ft container bookings for large volumes. Exclusive container space with direct routing and competitive rates.

  • 20ft & 40ft standard & high cube
  • Reefer & special equipment
  • Direct & transhipment routing
  • Competitive ocean freight rates
Clearance

Customs Clearance

Expert customs brokerage for import and export. We handle all documentation, duty calculations, and regulatory compliance on your behalf.

  • Bill of Entry preparation
  • Duty drawback assistance
  • GST & IGST documentation
  • DGFT & FSSAI compliance
Paperwork

Freight Documentation

Accurate and timely preparation of all shipping documents for smooth transit and hassle-free clearance at origin and destination.

  • Bill of Lading (OBL & Telex)
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Fumigation & phytosanitary certs
  • Packing list & commercial invoice
Transport

Inland Transportation

Reliable door-to-port trucking across Rajasthan and North India — seamless movement from your factory to the vessel.

  • Factory stuffing / destuffing
  • ICD to port transportation
  • Jodhpur to Mundra / JNPT
  • ODC & project cargo
